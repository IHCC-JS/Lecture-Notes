Calculating in a loop

On boot up you should see a `prompt()`. This prompt should ask you what operator you want to use. Then the though a `prompt()` the app will ask you for a number. Then the app will perform the operator of choice on the number given. Example below.

Output

  • Operator: +
  • Number: 4
